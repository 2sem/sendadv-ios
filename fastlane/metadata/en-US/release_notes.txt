Thanks for using Group SMS.

This update adds Send History. After you send a message, you can see when it was sent, which contact filters it went to, and how many people received it. Open it from the "Last sent" button on the main screen, and resend to the same contact filters from any record. If you already sent to the same contact filters within the last 24 hours, you will be reminded before you send again. Phone numbers are never saved.

We also made behind-the-scenes improvements for better stability.
